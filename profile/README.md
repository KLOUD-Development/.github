A one man team... yet still a team that's crazy passionate about self-built, quality of life products that help you gain control over your own day-to-day software.


## :sweden: The beginning
The start of the whole journey started when the head developer and founder, who wishes to stay anonymous, wanted to know how different simple programs worked from a young age. The ins- and outs of what goes on behind what we really see. He grew a passion for wanting to create his own tailormade software that help him in his every day life.



## :earth_africa: How it’s going
Through the years of independent developing, the founder built upon his expertise and became more precise, accurate and professional with his work. Building commercial-grade products and high-level GitHub repositories. After more than 8 years in the making, KLOUD has finally risen as a statement meant to stay. 


## Our products
Every line of code, every push, every commit, every error code. All by one person formed into an organization for you. Start using these open-source software [**here!**](https://github.com/fwlix0).

|--|--|

**LPRx**, a license plate reader tailormade for Swedish car enthusiasts. It's main purpose is to identify cars with a simple camera application. Take your phone out, open the **LPRx** app, point it at the license plate and *BOOM*. Make, model and color. Suspected law enforcement vehicle? **LPRx** will tell you if it is!
